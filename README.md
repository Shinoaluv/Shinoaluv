# Catherine Lin

I’m a student researcher and builder. I got interested in technology for small businesses through helping with my mom’s resale business and later working the takeout counter at a Chinese restaurant. These days, I interview people about how they use AI at work, build tools around what I learn, and work on robots with my VEX team.

[Explore my portfolio](https://catherines-portfolio.netlify.app/)

## Research and engineering

### BridgeBite · Technology for independent Asian restaurants

I started BridgeBite after testing a phone-ordering system that could take an order for “soup dumplings” but kept missing *xiaolongbao*. I interviewed restaurant owners and managers about dish names, accents, and the work of keeping menus current. Those conversations shape my CS IV capstone prototype, which focuses on multilingual menu editing that preserves original dish names, along with practical tools for tracking ingredient costs and food waste.

[Read the project overview](https://github.com/Shinoaluv/Asian-Restaurant-App)

### AI & Employment Research Initiative

I designed and conducted structured interviews with HR professionals and employees about hiring, changing job tasks, and emerging skill demands. I coded the interviews for recurring themes and built a website to share the findings. In a related literature review, I synthesized 40 sources and developed a framework for how responsibilities may shift across career stages. I also collaborated on an AI mediation prototype through UC Santa Cruz’s Science Internship Program.

[Explore the research site](https://ai-employment-research.netlify.app/)

### VEX Robotics · Team Sunny 1065A

As team captain, I help lead robot design, build planning, and match strategy. I’ve worked on an optical-sensor intake that sorts game pieces by color and a pneumatic power take-off for our drivetrain. I also mentor younger teams on design, programming, and engineering notebooks. Our team qualified for the VEX Robotics World Championship three consecutive times.

### DoubleS · Consumer hardware prototype

I started DoubleS to explore whether an item tracker could be thin enough to stick onto everyday objects and still be rechargeable. I’ve led early prototyping around Bluetooth Low Energy and a piezo buzzer and used consumer feedback to inform product decisions.

## Community initiatives

### Sound of the Future

I co-founded a youth-led nonprofit focused on hearing protection education and inclusion for Deaf and hard-of-hearing people. I lead outreach through school and community events, educational podcasts, and partnerships. Our team has grown to five U.S. chapters and two international chapters and distributed more than 1,500 care packages.

[Visit the nonprofit website](https://soundofthefuture.netlify.app/about) · [View the website source](https://github.com/Shinoaluv/SOTF-Website)

### Freedomframe · Photography and storytelling

I founded Freedomframe to document the work of women entrepreneurs, artists, and community leaders. I photograph and interview participants and recruit photographers, writers, and ambassadors to help share more stories from their communities.
