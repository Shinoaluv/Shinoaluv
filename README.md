# Catherine Lin

I’m a student researcher and builder interested in how technology changes the way people work and who gets to use it. My projects span AI research, hands-on engineering, and hearing health education.

## Selected projects

- **AI & Employment** — I study how AI changes tasks across career stages and use interviews to explore how people use AI at work. [Explore the research site](https://ai-employment-research.netlify.app/)
- **Sound of the Future** — I co-founded a youth-led nonprofit that teaches hearing protection and supports inclusion for Deaf and hard-of-hearing people. [Visit the project website](https://soundofthefuture.netlify.app/about) · [View source](https://github.com/Shinoaluv/SOTF-Website)
- **VEX Robotics · Team 1065A** — I captain Team Sunny and work on mechanisms and controls, including an optical-sensor color-sorting intake and a pneumatic power take-off for our drivetrain.
